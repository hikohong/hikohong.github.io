YouTube Ad Skip Auto-Clicker

Clicks the “Skip Ad” button the moment it appears, then gets you back to the video.

MIT licensed No tracking, no network calls Zero dependencies

Install · Chrome extension

No Tampermonkey, and no third-party userscript manager.

⬇ Download extension (.zip)
  1. Download the zip above and unzip it into a folder you won’t delete.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder you just unzipped.
  5. Reload YouTube — that’s it.
Why is there no one-click install? Google removed web-based extension installation entirely back in 2018, and any .crx from outside the Chrome Web Store is blocked by the browser. “Load unpacked” is the officially supported route, and the only way to install without publishing to the store. The upside is that everything stays on your own machine — nothing is uploaded and nothing goes through review.

What it does

  • Clicks “Skip Ad” — the instant the button becomes clickable.
  • Fast-forwards un-skippable ads — seeks them to the end. Only ever while an ad is playing; it never touches your actual video.
  • Mutes during ads, then restores your setting — YouTube reuses one player element for both ads and content, so the mute has to be undone or your video would stay silent.
  • Closes banner ads — the overlay ads along the bottom of the video.
  • Toolbar panel to toggle each feature, plus a count of ads skipped.
Requests exactly one permission, storage, for your toggles and the counter. No network calls, no data collection, no remote code. If you unmute manually part-way through an ad, it won’t fight you and mute it again.
